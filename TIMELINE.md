# Timeline

Dates come from the project's Git history and working notes. All of this was
built by directing AI coding agents (mainly Claude Code) against a written
specification.

## June 2026: the core exchange

- **9 June.** Project started: specification, agent definitions, CI.
- **9–12 June.** Ten build phases in four days: sign-up and roles, the market
  catalogue with ClinicalTrials.gov lookup, the trading engine (LMSR market
  maker), resolution and payout with a 48-hour challenge window, accuracy
  scoring and leaderboards, the web app and admin, live price updates,
  community features and abuse detection.
- **24 June.** Real money: a site-wide password gate, Google sign-up for invited
  users, USDC deposits proven to come from the user's own wallet, and
  withdrawals with two-factor approval.
- **28 June.** The discovery feed: a swipeable, personalised feed of markets.

## July 2026: depth

- Multi-outcome and range markets, trial details on each market page, and
  automatic source gathering.
- An order system with limit orders, a circuit breaker and freeze controls.
- Custody hardening: holds during incidents, audited releases.
- A community panel with a live trade tape and privacy for who traded.
- A new visual theme and home page.

## September 2026: safety and science

- Real-money custody reviewed and locked down: a multi-owner vault, recovery
  rehearsals, alert fences that refuse to start without a pager, private
  portfolio responses.
- A research pipeline reading trial registries and SEC filings, with an AI
  review of every drafted market question.
- **Pip**, the AI assistant on the Claude API, goes live.
- Company pages with share prices; insider-trading rules and declared conflicts.
- The core loop redesign: practice mode on famous past trials, typical odds,
  the career ladder, badges and progression.

## October 2026: telling the story

- Demo day: polished interface, a deposit bug fixed and 25 USDC credited, four
  real markets opened with company logos.
- The 5-minute founder pitch video, deck and ad for the UBC Venture Founder
  program.

## November 2026: speed, reach and testing

- Activity rewards, seasons, streaks and daily rounds.
- A redesigned admin console with search.
- Live motion: animated charts, price pulses and a recent-trades ticker.
- Open Google sign-up (money features stay invite-only).
- Daily closing-price markets and earnings markets built.
- Gifts between users; the first real gift went through on 15 November.
- A guided first-visit tour, live on 15 November.
- A native Android admin app with push alerts.
- **A test-bot swarm** that drives ten test accounts on the live site. On
  20 November eight bots made 266 real-money trades in ten minutes; every
  balance reconciled exactly. The bots also found real bugs, such as a market
  still listed as open after its close time.
- Legal research on where Endpoint can operate, ahead of a public launch.
