# Design explorations: "Specimen Plate"

A look study from 20–21 November 2026: eight sample screens (welcome, market,
desk, index, portfolio, catalysts, ranks, trade ticket) plus three wide
variants, in a natural-history "specimen plate" style. Illustrations were
generated with an image model.

**These are concepts only.** Nothing here is built in the app, and no decision
has been made to adopt it. The live look is in `../screenshots/app/`.
